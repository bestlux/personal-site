TerraForge is a long-horizon project for structured procedural geography.

## Pipeline focus

- irregular graph construction
- landmass and elevation synthesis
- hydrology and climate modeling
- biome, culture, and state generation layers

## Design goal

Generate worlds that are reproducible, testable, and expressive enough for downstream simulation and storytelling.
