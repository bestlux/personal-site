TerraForge is a long-horizon project for structured procedural geography.

## Goal

Create a single stop for DMs to generate and iterate full fantasy worlds end to end.

## Hardest challenge

Rendering and interacting with very large maps, especially around the 100k-cell scale.

## Status

Multiple worldgen approaches have been explored; none has fully stuck yet, and experimentation is ongoing.
