Terminal Atlas is where I test personal knowledge loops: build, observe, adapt, repeat.

## Why it exists

I wanted one place to map active experiments without pretending every idea is complete.

## What I learned

- Momentum improves when experiments are documented while unfinished.
- Small automation layers compound quickly.
- Structure beats intensity for long-term output.
