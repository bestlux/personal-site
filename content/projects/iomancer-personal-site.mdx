This site is intentionally treated as a product, not a one-off portfolio template.

## What it is

- a structured project stream
- a writing system with tags and RSS
- a monthly now-log for directional updates
- a privacy-conscious contact surface

## Why I built it this way

I wanted publication and reflection to be part of my normal workflow, not separate tasks I postpone.
