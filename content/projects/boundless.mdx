Boundless is an active systems project focused on reliable, low-latency multi-PC control.

## Problem

I wanted a local-first alternative to legacy keyboard/mouse sharing tools that feels instant and trustworthy in daily use.

## What I am building

- seamless cursor and keyboard handoff between machines
- resilient reconnect behavior after sleep and network changes
- workstation-friendly clipboard and transfer workflows

## Current status

Keyboard handoff is working well. Cursor handoff is the main active debugging track right now.
