Boundless is an active systems project focused on reliable, low-latency multi-PC control.

## Problem

I wanted a local-first alternative to legacy keyboard/mouse sharing tools that feels instant and stable during real daily use.

## What I am building

- edge-based cursor transfer between machines
- resilient reconnect behavior after sleep and network changes
- clipboard and file transfer flows designed for real workstation setups

## Why this project matters

This is where I explore systems-level UX: performance targets, recovery behavior, and practical operator ergonomics.
