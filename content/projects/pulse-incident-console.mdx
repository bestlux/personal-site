Pulse turns local Windows telemetry into actionable incident context.

## Current direction

- event log ingestion and incident detection pipelines
- severity modeling and local persistence
- AI-assisted explain workflows with history and UI integrations

## Why it exists

I wanted a focused operator-facing tool for triage that does not require a heavyweight external SOC stack.
